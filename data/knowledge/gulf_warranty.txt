# 海湾地区手机质保 / Gulf Region Phone Warranty

## 中文
- 标准质保：**2 年**（自购买日起），覆盖制造缺陷。
- 适用：海湾地区官方授权渠道购买的智能手机。
- 电池质保：**1 年**；充电器/耳机等配件 **3 个月**。
- 服务网点：阿联酋、沙特等主要城市设有授权服务中心。
- 不在质保范围：人为摔落、进水、自行拆机、非原装配件导致的损坏。
- 高温/沙尘环境：请避免长时间暴晒；接口进沙请勿强行插拔。

## English
- Standard warranty: **2 years** from purchase date for manufacturing defects.
- Applies to smartphones bought via authorized Gulf channels.
- Battery: **1 year**; charger/earbuds accessories **3 months**.
- Service centers in major UAE and KSA cities.
- Excluded: drops, liquid damage, unauthorized repair, non-genuine accessories.
- Hot/dusty climate: avoid prolonged sun exposure; do not force connectors if sand is present.

## 延保 / Extended Warranty
可选延长质保至 3 年，购买时或标准质保期内通过官网或服务中心办理。

Optional 3-year extended warranty available at purchase or during standard warranty period.
