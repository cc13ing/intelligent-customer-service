# Cross-Border Shipping to Gulf (English)

## Delivery Times
- UAE / Saudi Arabia: 7–14 business days (standard), 3–5 days (express).
- Kuwait / Qatar / Bahrain / Oman: 10–18 business days.
- Times exclude customs clearance delays.

## Carriers
DHL Express, Aramex, SMSA, and local partners for last-mile delivery.

## Tracking
Tracking number sent by email/SMS once shipped. Track on carrier website or in "My Orders".

## Customs & Duties
Recipient may pay import VAT (typically 5–15% depending on country). Some promotions include DDP (duties prepaid).

## Shipping Issues
If tracking shows no update for 7+ days, contact support with order number. Lost packages: full refund or replacement after investigation (up to 15 business days).
