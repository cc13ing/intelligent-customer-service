# Gulf Region Phone Warranty (English)

## Standard Warranty
- Duration: **2 years** from purchase date for manufacturing defects.
- Coverage: smartphones purchased through authorized Gulf region channels (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman).
- Battery warranty: **1 year** from purchase date.
- Accessories (charger, earbuds): **3 months**.

## Service Centers
Authorized service centers in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam, Kuwait City, Doha, Manama, and Muscat.

## Exclusions
Not covered: accidental drops, liquid damage, unauthorized repair, use of non-genuine accessories, cosmetic wear, software issues from third-party apps.

## Climate Notes (Gulf)
Avoid prolonged exposure to direct sunlight (50°C+). If sand enters charging port, do not force connectors; visit a service center.

## Extended Warranty
Optional 3-year extended warranty available at purchase or during the standard warranty period via official website or service center.
