# Payment & Currency (English)

## Accepted Payment Methods
- Credit/debit cards: Visa, Mastercard, Amex.
- Digital wallets: Apple Pay, Google Pay (where supported).
- Local options: Mada (Saudi Arabia), KNET (Kuwait) in selected stores.

## Currencies
Prices displayed in USD or local currency (AED, SAR). Bank may apply FX fees for non-local cards.

## Invoices
VAT-compliant invoice emailed after payment. Request duplicate invoice via support with order number.

## Failed Payments
If charged but order not created, refund auto-initiated within 48 hours. Contact support with bank reference.
