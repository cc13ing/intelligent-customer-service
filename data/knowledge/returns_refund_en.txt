# Returns & Refunds (English)

## Return Window
- 14 days from delivery for unused items in original packaging.
- Defective items: 30 days for replacement or refund.

## How to Return
1. Log in and open "My Orders".
2. Select the order and click "Return / Refund".
3. Print prepaid label (Gulf regions) or use provided return address.
4. Refund processed within 5–10 business days after warehouse receipt.

## Non-Returnable
Opened software licenses, personalized items, hygiene products if seal broken.

## Refund Method
Original payment method. Bank transfers may take 3–5 extra business days.

## Exchange
Same-SKU exchange available within return window; contact support for different model.
