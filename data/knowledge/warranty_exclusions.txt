# 质保除外条款 / Warranty Exclusions

## 中文
- 以下情况不在质保范围：
  - 人为损坏（摔落、进水、私自拆机）
  - 非授权维修或改装
  - 正常磨损（电池容量衰减、屏幕划痕）
  - 序列号被篡改或无法识别
  - 因使用非原装配件导致的故障
- 电池：质保期内容量低于 80% 可申请检测更换（需诊断确认）。

## English
- Not covered: accidental damage, water ingress, unauthorized repair/modification,
  normal wear (battery degradation, scratches), tampered serial number,
  failures caused by non-genuine accessories.
- Battery: replacement if capacity below 80% during warranty (diagnosis required).
