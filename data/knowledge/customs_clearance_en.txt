# Customs Clearance – Gulf (English)

## Process
Package clears customs in destination country. Typical clearance: 1–5 business days (UAE/Saudi often faster).

## Required Documents
Commercial invoice and airway bill handled by carrier. Recipient may need national ID or importer code (business orders).

## Delays
Peak seasons (Ramadan, National Day sales) may add 2–3 days. Customs holds: carrier will contact recipient.

## Restricted Items
Check local telecom approval for phones/modems. Batteries must meet IATA shipping rules.
